Panora 6.62 — partner mobile product-photo stability

Includes the 6.61 mobile bake/delivery-date checkout fix.

Partner storefront / cabinet
- product photos no longer blink during background cloud polling on mobile;
- public-catalog rows are canonicalized by product id before change detection, so changing Supabase row order does not look like a product change;
- partner catalog/prices are canonicalized before comparison, preventing false “price/product changed” events every 2.5 seconds;
- background pricing refresh no longer directly rebuilds the entire partner cabinet;
- the New Order and Profile tabs are protected from background workspace rebuilds while they are open;
- catalog DOM is rebuilt only when visual product data actually changes;
- price-only changes update price/tier text without replacing product <img> nodes;
- cart/workspace renders are skipped when there is no meaningful catalog change;
- duplicate legacy pricing listeners that rebuilt storefront/workspace are removed;
- mobile product images have no opacity transition.

Result: normal background polling continues, but existing image DOM nodes stay mounted and loaded.

No SQL required beyond the already-required Panora 6.60 communication/Push migration.
