Panora 6.34 — in-app retail messages and fallback channels

- Main customer communication channel is now inside Panora order page.
- Bakery can write to a customer from Retail orders; customer can reply without registration through the protected order token.
- Message read state is tracked separately for bakery and customer.
- Backup contact channels are configurable: WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS and Email.
- Backup channels do not replace Panora messaging and do not affect order, stock, production or finance status.
- Existing 6.33 event journal remains separate from human conversation.
- Push notifications are intentionally not enabled yet; 6.34 provides the message/event source for future Web Push.
- Mobile admin and retail-order layouts are adapted for messaging.
- PWA build 6340.
