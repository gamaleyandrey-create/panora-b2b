Panora 6.47

Orders local-cache quota hardening (partner + bakery):
- all partner writes of panora-orders are routed through the bounded panora-portal-orders cache;
- all bakery writes of panora-orders use a quota-safe bounded cache;
- working orders remain locally available; only the most recent 20 archived orders are cached;
- full history remains in Supabase and is loaded from cloud;
- cloud status/order actions do not fail merely because localStorage is full;
- QuotaExceededError is not treated as a cloud synchronization failure;
- cart, auth/session, messages, inventory movements and cloud data are untouched;
- B2B 1–7 retail / 8+ wholesale pricing rule remains unchanged;
- Partner Push from 6.44 remains intact.
