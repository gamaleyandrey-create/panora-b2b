Panora 6.83 — production and finished-stock loss accounting

Build: 6830
Package series: 8

Changes:
- factual bake waste now appears automatically in Finance as a production loss valued at raw-material cost;
- explicit finished-bread “Write-off / waste” stock movements now appear automatically as stock losses;

- inventory and technical +/- corrections remain reconciliation entries and are not auto-expensed;
- automatic loss rows are visible in the Finance expense table and cannot be edited or deleted there;
- Finance refreshes immediately after bake-completion or finished-stock movement changes.
