Panora 6.97 — B2B reopened-note archive settlement date consistency

Build: 6970
Package series: 8

Changes:
- bakery partner-account archive now sorts closed delivery notes by the actual settlement/close moment, not by the original delivery date;
- a delivery note reopened after a dispute/cancellation and later paid again returns to the archive with its new real close date;
- archive cards explicitly show the settlement close date;
- closed B2B rows now say “Расчёты закрыты” instead of “Оплачено полностью”, because a note can be settled partly or fully by a non-cash goods-return credit;
- payment/return allocation semantics remain unchanged.

No layout redesign. No new SQL.
