Panora 6.64 — partner orders only on real bake days

Includes all fixes through 6.63.

Partner date rules
- partner order date is always a real open BAKE date from the bakery bake_days calendar;
- a date is available only when EVERY product currently in the cart is scheduled in bake_items for that bake day;
- ordering cutoff must still be open for every selected product/day;
- the mobile Partner → New Order date selector recalculates immediately when product quantities change;
- if selected products have no common bake day, checkout is disabled and Panora shows “Нет общего дня выпечки”;
- cart and checkout date selectors are the same compatible bake-date list;
- labels explicitly distinguish “Выпечка” from “Поставка”;
- checkout never silently moves an invalid date to another arbitrary day;
- the final submit validates the complete cart again immediately before calling Supabase.

Server hardening (new SQL)
- panora_create_order accepts only bake_days.accepting_orders=true;
- cutoff_at must still be in the future;
- bake_date must not be in the past in Europe/Madrid;
- every ordered product must exist in bake_items for the selected bake_day;
- delivery date stored on the order comes from bake_days.delivery_date, not from a client-supplied arbitrary date.

Requires:
- SUPABASE_PANORA_6_64_PARTNER_BAKE_DAY_GUARD.sql

No Edge Function changes. Panora 6.60 communication/Push migration remains unchanged.
