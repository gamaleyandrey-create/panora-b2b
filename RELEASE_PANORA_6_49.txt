Panora 6.49

Unified raw-material purchase prices:
- every ingredient has one purchase price for the whole system;
- the same price feeds Recipe cost, Purchasing, Raw Materials stock value and Finance;
- new recipe ingredients immediately show “Цена не указана” until a purchase price is entered;
- purchase price can be entered directly in the recipe editor, in Purchasing or in Raw Materials — all edit the same value;
- g is priced per kg, ml per liter, pcs per piece;
- semi-finished ingredients use the purchase price of their source raw material;
- ingredient prices are synchronized through Supabase raw_material_prices for multi-device work;
- existing local panora-ingredient-costs values are migrated to cloud on first sync if the cloud table is empty;
- no recipe quantity, stock movement or historical bake snapshot is rewritten.
