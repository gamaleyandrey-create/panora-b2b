REPORTS + EDO
- Added a dedicated Bakery > Finance > Reports module without changing Partner or Retail UI.
- Reports include Overview, Sales, Production, Finished Stock, Finance, Partners, Quality, Legal registers and EDO.
- Period presets and explicit From/To filters are available together with channel, partner and product filters.
- KPI header shows revenue, sold pieces, good production, bake waste, current receivables and EDO items needing attention.
- Mandatory/control register dashboard covers Facturas expedidas, Facturas recibidas, IVA control, traceability, APPCC/HACCP, Registro de jornada source availability, packaging, food waste/write-offs, EDO and VERI*FACTU integration state.
- The legal dashboard is deliberately a completeness/source control: it does not claim that a green card replaces gestor/asesor review or an AEAT filing.
- Added EDO / Factura electronica tracking for outgoing B2B documents with lifecycle status, channel and external identifier. Statuses are never presented as externally sent unless the operator records the confirmed status.
- Added a structured incoming electronic-invoice register with supplier, NIF/CIF, taxable base, IVA, total, channel, external ID and lifecycle status.
- CSV/Excel-compatible export and Print/PDF are available for the currently open report.
- Existing Finance, Payments, Production & Safety, label editor and stock workflows are preserved.

MANDATORY SHELF-PRICE BLOCKS
- Spain shelf-price checks use separate mandatory blocks: Denominación ES, Precio de venta, Precio por kg, Peso de la pieza, Alérgenos EU-14, conditional QUID and >24 h pan común warning.
- No BIO marking.
- No SQL migration required.


SAMPLE / TRIAL LABELS + MUNBYN RW403B
- Added a dedicated “Образец” template for bakery trials and tastings. Multiple variants can use the same base bread while recording different baking methods and ingredient/proportion changes.
- Sample labels print MUESTRA / PRUEBA / DEGUSTACIÓN / TEST DE HORNEADO, an explicit NO VENTA mark, variant code, bake method, change note, bake date, weight and allergens. Price and €/kg are never printed in sample mode; QR and EAN-13 stay optional.
- Added MUNBYN RW403B 203 dpi thermal profile with 40–110 mm width guardrail, monochrome output and exact-size roll printing at 100%.
- Fixed the preview header so its explanatory text wraps below the 1:1/physical-size line instead of overlapping.
- Shelf QUID detection now also checks declared claims/emphasis, not only the product name.
- Mandatory shelf checklist wording is explicit: Denominación ES, Precio de venta, Precio por kg, Peso de la pieza, Alérgenos EU-14, conditional QUID, and conditional pan común >24 h warning.
- No BIO marking. No SQL migration required.
