Panora 6.91 — delivery-note-specific payment reminder reopening

Build: 6910
Package series: 8

Changes:
- payment reminder re-opening is now evaluated per delivery note instead of per partner account;
- a disputed or cancelled payment no longer resurrects already-sent reminders for unrelated delivery notes whose balance did not change;
- FIFO side effects are covered: if cancelling/disputing one payment really reopens another delivery note through payment reallocation, that note correctly becomes reminder-eligible again;
- linked overpayments are handled the same way, including overflow applied to later delivery notes;
- legacy reminder records remain supported, while modern records continue to use the stored debt snapshot as the primary stale-reminder guard.

No layout changes. No new SQL.
