Panora 6.77 — regression fixes

Build: 6770
Package series: 8

Changes:
- B2B confirm/cancel no longer pretend to succeed locally when Supabase is configured but not ready;
- critical B2B status transitions now wait for server confirmation before changing the UI state;
- pending B2B payments are no longer treated as confirmed when loaded from Supabase;
- partner debt, paid totals and payment allocation exclude pending payments until confirmation;
- pending payments remain visible in operation history and can still be explicitly confirmed.

No retail workflow, mobile-layout or desktop-layout changes in 6.77.
No new SQL in 6.77.
