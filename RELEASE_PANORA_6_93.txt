Panora 6.93 — advance settlement chronology

Build: 6930
Package series: 8

Changes:
- an advance / overpayment still automatically covers the oldest open delivery notes and future B2B delivery notes;
- when a future DN is fully covered by an older advance, its archive close date can no longer precede the DN itself;
- bakery-side payment allocation details use the real application date when an older payment later covers a newer DN;
- partner operation history now shows a real advance / overpayment after an operation instead of flattening negative settlement balance to zero;
- fully covered DNs remain excluded from payment reminders, so this chronology fix does not create false reminders.

No layout redesign. No new SQL.
