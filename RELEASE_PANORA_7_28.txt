Panora 7.28 — product card deletion and recoverable archive

- “Карточки продукции” now shows only active product cards in the main grid.
- “Удалить карточку” removes the product from active bakery catalog, partner/new orders, storefront and bake planning after confirmed Supabase save.

- Historical orders, delivery notes, prices and product references are preserved; no physical DELETE is performed.

- No SQL/schema change is required.
