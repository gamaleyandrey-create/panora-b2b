Panora 7.30 — bakery payment acceptance reliability

- Bakery “Accept payment” now completes the whole acceptance flow: an admin-entered payment is confirmed before the UI reports success, so it immediately reduces debt.
- If legacy panora_record_payment / panora_confirm_payment RPCs are absent from Supabase, the bakery has a verified admin-table fallback instead of a dead button.
- The fallback is used only for missing-RPC/schema-cache errors; validation/auth errors are never bypassed.
- Payment confirmation is verified from the returned Supabase row before local finance is updated.
- Payment amount parsing accepts the localized decimal parser used elsewhere in Panora.
- No SQL/schema change is required.
