SPAIN BREAD / BAKERY LABEL COMPLIANCE
- Added an in-app Spanish legal filling guide for shelf price tags, unpackaged bread, retail-immediate packaging and ordinary prepacked products.
- Product label metadata now includes bread category, <=24 h bakery mode, legal-claim confirmations, largest package surface, nutrition exemption flag, origin/primary-ingredient origin, and packaging-waste information.
- Spanish date wording uses “Consumir preferentemente antes del …” when a day is printed. Bakery/repostería normally consumed within 24 h can omit the minimum-durability date when the product is explicitly marked in that mode.
- Lote is no longer requested as an absolute duplicate when a clearly printed date contains day and month; Panora warns only when neither adequate date nor lot identifies the batch.
- QUID checking now also applies to shelf tags where the product name or emphasis requires a percentage.
- Pan común gets a 24-hour sale-age warning based on the baking date. Claims such as artesana, masa madre and larga fermentación are checked against explicit confirmations before printing.
- Prepacked nutrition can use the Annex V small-direct-local-supply exemption only after explicit confirmation; otherwise the full nutrition declaration remains expected.
- Country of origin is not printed automatically. When origin is declared and the primary ingredient differs, Panora can print the primary ingredient origin statement.
- Added household packaging waste/container information, point-of-sale mode, and compostable-packaging certification/no-litter checks.
- Added approximate x-height guardrails for edited mandatory packaging blocks: 1.2 mm, or 0.9 mm when the recorded largest package surface is below 80 cm².
- Missing legal/product fields remain overridable through the existing “ignore warnings and print” confirmation; only technical layout failures remain hard blocks.
- No BIO marking. No SQL migration required.
