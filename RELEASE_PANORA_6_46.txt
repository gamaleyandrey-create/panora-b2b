Panora 6.46

Partner portal local-storage quota fix.
- panora-portal-orders uses a bounded compact local cache;
- active/working orders are preserved locally;
- only the 20 most recent archived orders are kept in the fallback cache;
- heavy nested fields are removed from the local fallback copy only;
- full order history remains in Supabase;
- QuotaExceededError is no longer reported as a cloud synchronization error;
- cart, authentication/session, messages, inventory movements and cloud data are not removed;
- Partner Push UI from 6.44 remains enabled on desktop and mobile.
