Panora 6.86 — disputed-payment accounting hardening

Build: 6860
Package series: 8

Changes:
- an open B2B payment dispute no longer closes delivery-note debt or creates advance/credit;
- disputed payments remain visible in history but are suspended from FIFO allocation and balance calculations;
- overdue/payment reminders use the reopened debt produced by the shared finance allocation;
- bakery account history now shows disputed payments explicitly;
- bakery can resolve a dispute with server-confirmed actions: keep payment as valid or cancel it;
- dispute decisions never change local debt until Supabase confirms the payment row state.

No layout redesign in 6.86.
No new SQL in 6.86.
