B2B local economic date fix:
- Supabase payment timestamps are converted from UTC to the bakery-local calendar day before admin accounting uses them;
- partner payment history uses the same local-day rule, so a payment just after local midnight no longer appears on the previous UTC day;
- payment reversal/dispute event dates inherit the same local economic-date conversion;
- delivery-note cloud mapping uses the same helper for consistency while existing noon-based DN dates remain unchanged.

No SQL changes.
