Panora 6.87 — payment-reminder debt-cycle hardening

Build: 6870
Package series: 8

Changes:
- a payment reminder marked as sent becomes actionable again when the confirmed outstanding balance changes;
- partial payments no longer leave a stale already-sent reminder with the old amount;
- a disputed or cancelled B2B payment that reopens debt creates a fresh reminder cycle even if the same delivery note was reminded before;
- unchanged debt does not generate duplicate reminder cycles;
- legacy reminder records remain compatible and are invalidated only when a newer payment state change occurred.

No layout redesign in 6.87.
No new SQL in 6.87.
