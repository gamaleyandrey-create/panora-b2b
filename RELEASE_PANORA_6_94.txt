Panora 6.94 — advance-aware payment reminders at startup

Build: 6940
Package series: 8

Changes:
- B2B payment reminders calculate the real outstanding DN balance locally even before cloud-sync.js finishes loading;
- an existing advance / overpayment is therefore deducted immediately from a newly created or cached DN reminder (for example 100 € DN - 30 € advance = 70 € reminder);
- reminder state snapshots use the same local FIFO allocation instead of stale note.paid values;
- persisted B2B return-credit payments are included in the pre-cloud fallback until the authoritative effective-return totals are available;
- once cloud finance is ready, the same shared allocation remains authoritative.

No layout redesign. No new SQL.
