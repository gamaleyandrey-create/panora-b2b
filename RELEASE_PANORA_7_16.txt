Panora 7.16 — direct bakery payment cancellation

- Bakery can now cancel an erroneously recorded ordinary confirmed B2B payment directly from the partner account history.
- Cancellation immediately reopens the affected debt using the existing verified allocation logic.
- Return-credit payments cannot be cancelled with this action.
- Partner live polling from 7.15 continues to receive the cancelled status automatically, so the reopened debt appears without manual reload.
- No schema or SQL change is required.
- Archive set: Panora 9.1.
