Panora 6.65 — bakery bake calendar active/history split

Includes all fixes through 6.64.

Bakery bake calendar
- the normal “Календарь выпечки” is now a working calendar: today + future only;
- past bake dates are NOT deleted from Supabase/local history;
- past dates automatically move to a read-only “Прошедшие” mode after the bakery day changes;
- “Сегодня / Будущие” returns to the working calendar;
- the active calendar cannot navigate into months earlier than the current bakery month;
- past cells inside the current month are hidden from the working view;
- history mode shows only dates before today and does not allow editing/cancelling schedule from the calendar;
- historical cards are marked “Только просмотр”;
- summaries in the calendar follow the current mode (active vs history);
- the plan list under the calendar follows the same active/history mode;
- bakery-local “today” is resolved in Europe/Madrid, not UTC.

No data is physically removed. Old bake_days, orders, production facts, raw-material movements and documents remain available for accounting/history.

No SQL required. Panora 6.64 server bake-day guard remains required.
