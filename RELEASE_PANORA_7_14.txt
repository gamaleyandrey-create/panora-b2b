Panora 7.14 — retail return caps aligned across physical stock and Finance FIFO

- Linked retail return movements are capped by the original sold quantity inside the finished-stock ledger.
- Duplicate/stale over-return movements can no longer create phantom returned bread.
- Finance FIFO applies the same ceiling, so later sales and write-offs cannot consume extra returned cost layers.
- Multi-product partial returns keep independent product quantities and historical sale COGS.
- No SQL changes.
